Welcome

We are Kingsbury Methodist Church, a Global Methodist congregation in the small community of Kingsbury, Texas, and we invite all to join us for worship.

If you are looking for a family-friendly church, come visit us on Sunday at 9:30 AM. Come as you are — there is a seat for you.

Service Times

Sunday Worship

9:30 AM

Every Sunday morning in the sanctuary at 7035 FM 2438.

Your First Visit

No dress code and no need to sign in. Arrive a few minutes early and someone will help you find a seat.
